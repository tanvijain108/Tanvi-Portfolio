A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

About brand & content.

Do you create a brand identity from scratch?+

The focus is applying an existing or emerging identity consistently across digital content. Full identity creation can be discussed separately.

Which channels can you design for?+

Websites and social media, including posts, carousels and reels, plus campaign creatives and marketing layouts.

Can you design a regular content series?+

Yes. A set of templates makes it easier to keep a series consistent and quick to produce.

Do you also write the content?+

The service covers the design. Copy can be provided by your team, or drafted together with AI assistance and approved by you.
