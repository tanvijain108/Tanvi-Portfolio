Project overview

StreamBot is a conversational movie recommendation chatbot that helps users discover personalized content across OTT platforms based on their mood, genre preferences, language, and viewing interests. It simplifies content discovery, reduces decision fatigue, and provides quick, relevant recommendations through an interactive chat-based experience.
